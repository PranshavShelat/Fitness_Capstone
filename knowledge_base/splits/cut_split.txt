Goal: Cutting (fat loss while preserving muscle)

Training strategy: Train close to full-body frequency (each muscle group hit 2-3x/week) since a calorie deficit makes it harder to recover from high per-session volume, but total weekly volume should stay high enough to signal the body to keep muscle. Favor higher rep ranges (15-20) with shorter rest periods (45-60s) to keep sessions efficient and metabolically demanding, and add extra core/conditioning work (planks, russian twists) since it's cheap on recovery.

Suggested weekly structure using this app's tracked exercises: alternate a "Push" day (Pushups, Shoulder Press, Tricep Dips, Lateral Raises), a "Pull" day (Pullups, Bicep Curls, Hammer Curls), and a "Legs & Core" day (Squats, Planks, Russian Twists), repeating the cycle across the week with 1-2 rest or light-cardio days. 5-6 training days per week is reasonable since session intensity (weight) is lower during a cut, keeping recovery demand manageable.
