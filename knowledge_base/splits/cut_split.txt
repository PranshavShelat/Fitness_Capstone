Goal: Cutting (fat loss while preserving muscle)

Training strategy: Fat loss demands preserving as much muscle as possible while running a calorie deficit, so a Push/Pull/Legs (PPL) split repeated twice across the week keeps training frequency high (each muscle group hit twice weekly), which better protects muscle during a deficit than training each group only once. Favor higher rep ranges (12-20) with shorter rest periods (45-60s) to keep sessions efficient and metabolically demanding.

Suggested weekly structure, covering every muscle group properly each time it comes up:
- Push (chest/shoulders/triceps): pick 4-5 from Bench Press, Inclined Bench Press, Decline Bench Press, Shoulder Press, Pushups, Chest Fly Machine, Tricep Dips, Tricep Pushdown, Lateral Raises.
- Pull (back/biceps): pick 4-5 from Pullups, Lat Pulldown, T-Bar Row, Bicep Curls, Hammer Curls.
- Legs & Core: a complete leg day always needs a quad-dominant lift (Squats or Leg Extension), a hamstring/glute-dominant lift (Deadlift, Romanian Deadlift, or Hip Thrust), and core work (Planks, Russian Twists, or Leg Raises) - never just one leg exercise carrying the whole day.

Run Push/Pull/Legs twice through the week (6 training days) with 1 rest day. Vary which specific exercises fill each day's slots across the two passes rather than repeating the exact same 3-4 exercises both times - the point of a 6-day cycle is broader coverage, not doing the same short list twice.
