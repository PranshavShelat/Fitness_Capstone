Goal: Bulking (building muscle in a calorie surplus)

Training strategy: A calorie surplus supports more recovery, so sessions can push heavier loads at lower rep ranges (6-12) with longer rest (90-120s) between sets to maximize strength and hypertrophy stimulus. A Push/Pull/Legs (PPL) split repeated twice across the week, with a rest day worked in, gives each muscle group two sessions weekly at high intensity while still recovering between repeats.

Suggested weekly structure, covering every muscle group properly each time it comes up:
- Push (chest/shoulders/triceps): pick 4-5 from Bench Press, Inclined Bench Press, Decline Bench Press, Shoulder Press, Pushups, Chest Fly Machine, Tricep Dips, Tricep Pushdown, Lateral Raises.
- Pull (back/biceps): pick 4-5 from Pullups, Lat Pulldown, T-Bar Row, Bicep Curls, Hammer Curls.
- Legs & Core: a complete leg day always needs a quad-dominant lift (Squats or Leg Extension), a hamstring/glute-dominant lift (Deadlift, Romanian Deadlift, or Hip Thrust), and core work (Planks, Russian Twists, or Leg Raises) - never just one leg exercise carrying the whole day.

Run Push/Pull/Legs twice through the week (6 training days) with 1 full rest day, since the surplus supports the extra recovery demand of training each muscle group twice weekly at higher intensity. Vary which specific exercises fill each day's slots across the two passes rather than repeating the exact same short list twice - e.g. Bench Press on the first push day, Inclined Bench Press and Decline Bench Press on the second.
