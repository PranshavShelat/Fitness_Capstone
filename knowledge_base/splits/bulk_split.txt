Goal: Bulking (building muscle in a calorie surplus)

Training strategy: A calorie surplus supports more recovery, so sessions can push heavier loads at lower rep ranges (8-12) with longer rest (90-120s) between sets to maximize strength and hypertrophy stimulus. A push/pull/legs split with a dedicated rest day between repeats of the same muscle group gives each session enough recovery to keep progressively adding weight or reps week to week.

Suggested weekly structure using this app's tracked exercises: a "Push" day (Shoulder Press, Pushups, Tricep Dips, Lateral Raises), a "Pull" day (Pullups, Bicep Curls, Hammer Curls), a "Legs & Core" day (Squats, Planks, Russian Twists), then repeat the cycle once more across the week (6 training days) with 1 full rest day, since the surplus supports the extra recovery demand of training each muscle group twice weekly at higher intensity.
