Goal: Maintaining (holding current weight and muscle, general fitness)

Training strategy: A balanced push/pull/legs split at moderate rep ranges (10-15) and moderate rest (60-90s) is enough to maintain muscle and cardiovascular fitness without the extra recovery demand of a bulk or the elevated volume of a cut. Consistency across the week matters more than pushing intensity in any single session.

Suggested weekly structure using this app's tracked exercises: a "Push" day (Pushups, Shoulder Press, Tricep Dips, Lateral Raises), a "Pull" day (Pullups, Bicep Curls, Hammer Curls), and a "Legs & Core" day (Squats, Planks, Russian Twists), 3-4 training days per week with rest days in between, repeating the same three-day rotation.
