Exercise: Deadlift
Category: Legs (hamstrings, glutes, lower back, quads)
Type: Compound, barbell

The conventional deadlift is one of the most complete compound lifts, loading the entire posterior chain (hamstrings, glutes, lower back) along with the quads and grip. It anchors a leg day as the heaviest, most demanding lift and should be done first while fresh.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 10-15 reps per set, 3 sets, moderate rest (90s) - technique matters more than load here even at higher reps.
- Maintaining: 6-10 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 4-6 reps per set with heavier loads, 4 sets, long rest (120-180s).

Common form cue: keep the bar close to the shins/legs throughout the pull and maintain a neutral (flat) spine rather than rounding the lower back - a rounded back under a heavy pulled load is the single biggest injury risk with this lift.
