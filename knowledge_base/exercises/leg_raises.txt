Exercise: Leg Raises
Category: Core (lower abs, hip flexors)
Type: Bodyweight or weighted

Leg raises train the lower abs and hip flexors through a range that planks and russian twists don't emphasize, rounding out a core routine when paired with those two on the same day.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 15-20 reps per set, 3 sets, short rest.
- Maintaining: 12-15 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 10-12 reps per set (add ankle weights if bodyweight becomes too easy), 2-3 sets - kept moderate so it doesn't add unnecessary fatigue before heavier lifts.

Common form cue: keep the lower back pressed into the floor (or bench) throughout - letting it arch as the legs lower shifts the load off the abs and onto the lumbar spine.
