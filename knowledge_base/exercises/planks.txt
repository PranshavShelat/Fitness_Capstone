Exercise: Planks
Category: Core (rectus abdominis, transverse abdominis, obliques)
Type: Isometric hold, bodyweight

Planks build core endurance and spinal stability without spinal flexion, making them a low-risk way to train the core on any day, including rest/active-recovery days.

Hold guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 3-4 holds, building toward 45-60s each - core work is cheap on recovery, so it can be done more frequently during a cut.
- Maintaining: 3 holds of 30-45s.
- Bulking: 2-3 holds of 30-45s - kept brief so it doesn't eat into recovery needed for heavier compound lifts elsewhere in the split.

Form risk this app watches for: the hips sagging below a straight line, which forces the lower back into hyperextension. Squeezing the glutes and bracing the abs to keep a straight line from shoulders to ankles avoids this.
