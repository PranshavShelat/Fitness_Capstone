Exercise: Squats
Category: Legs (quadriceps, glutes, hamstrings)
Type: Compound, bodyweight or loaded

Squats are a foundational lower-body compound movement, training the quadriceps, glutes, and hamstrings together while also demanding core stability. They belong on any leg day or full-body day regardless of goal.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 15-20 reps per set, 3-4 sets, shorter rest (45-60s) to keep heart rate elevated and preserve muscle in a calorie deficit.
- Maintaining: 10-15 reps per set, 3 sets, moderate rest (60-90s).
- Bulking: 8-12 reps per set, 4 sets, longer rest (90-120s) to allow heavier loading and maximize strength/hypertrophy stimulus.

Form risk this app watches for: knees caving inward (valgus) under load, and descending so deep the pelvis tucks under and loads the lower spine. Keeping the knees tracking over the toes and stopping depth at a stable pelvis position avoids both.
