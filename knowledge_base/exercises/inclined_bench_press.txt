Exercise: Inclined Bench Press
Category: Push (upper chest, front shoulders, triceps)
Type: Compound, barbell or dumbbell

The incline press shifts emphasis onto the upper chest and front shoulders compared to a flat press, and pairs well on the same push day as a flat or decline press to cover the whole chest.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 12-15 reps per set, 3 sets, moderate rest (60s).
- Maintaining: 8-12 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 6-10 reps per set, 3-4 sets, longer rest (90s).

Common form cue: keep the incline moderate (30-45 degrees) - too steep a bench turns the movement into more of a shoulder press than a chest press, shifting load off the intended muscle.
