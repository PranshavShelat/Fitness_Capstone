Exercise: T-Bar Row
Category: Pull (mid-back, lats, biceps)
Type: Compound, barbell/landmine

The T-bar row is a horizontal pulling movement that builds mid-back thickness in a way vertical pulls (pullups, lat pulldown) don't emphasize as much, making it a strong second pulling movement on pull day.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 15-20 reps per set, 3 sets, moderate rest (60s).
- Maintaining: 10-15 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 6-10 reps per set with heavier loads, 4 sets, longer rest (90-120s).

Common form cue: hinge at the hips with a flat back and pull the weight into the lower ribs/upper abdomen, rather than rounding the lower back to generate momentum - a flat back protects the spine under load.
