Exercise: Hammer Curls
Category: Pull accessory (biceps, brachialis, forearms)
Type: Isolation, dumbbell

Hammer curls use a neutral (palms-facing-in) grip, shifting emphasis onto the brachialis and forearms alongside the biceps. Pairing hammer curls with regular bicep curls on the same arm day gives more complete arm development than either alone.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 15-20 reps per set, 3 sets, short rest (30-45s).
- Maintaining: 10-15 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 8-12 reps per set with heavier dumbbells, 3-4 sets, rest 60s.

Form risk this app watches for: the elbow drifting forward away from the torso, same failure mode as the regular bicep curl - keeping the elbow at the side isolates the arm rather than turning the movement into a front raise.
