Exercise: Lat Pulldown
Category: Pull (lats, upper back, biceps)
Type: Compound, cable machine

The lat pulldown is a machine-assisted alternative to pullups, building the same lats and upper back while allowing precise, scalable loading - a strong main lift for pull day, especially for building toward unassisted pullups.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 15-20 reps per set, 3 sets, short rest (60s).
- Maintaining: 10-15 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 8-12 reps per set with heavier loads, 4 sets, longer rest (90s).

Common form cue: pull the bar to the upper chest by driving the elbows down and back, not by leaning back excessively or yanking with momentum - a controlled pull keeps the tension on the lats.
