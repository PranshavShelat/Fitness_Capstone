Exercise: Russian Twists
Category: Core (obliques, rectus abdominis)
Type: Rotational, bodyweight or loaded

Russian twists train rotational core strength through the obliques, complementing the anti-extension work of planks. They fit well on core-focused days or as a finisher after a main lift.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 20-30 total twists (10-15 per side), 3 sets, short rest.
- Maintaining: 16-20 total twists, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 16-20 total twists with a held weight/medicine ball, 2-3 sets - kept moderate so it doesn't add unnecessary fatigue before heavier lifts.

Controlled rotation through the torso, not just swinging the arms, keeps the load on the obliques rather than momentum, and keeping the feet anchored or lightly floating (not slamming down) protects the lower back.
