Exercise: Shoulder Press
Category: Push (front/medial shoulder, triceps)
Type: Compound, dumbbell

The shoulder press is the primary vertical pressing movement and should anchor push/shoulder day, performed before the smaller isolation accessories like lateral raises.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 15-20 reps per set, 3 sets, light-moderate weight, short rest.
- Maintaining: 10-15 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 8-12 reps per set with heavier dumbbells, 4 sets, longer rest (90-120s).

Form risks this app watches for: the torso leaning forward (turning part of the press into a forward push rather than vertical, loading the lower back), the wrists dropping to or below elbow height (a weak "chicken wing" position), and the elbows rising up and out to the sides before the arm has actually extended - closer to a lateral raise than a true overhead press, loading the shoulder in a wide, unsupported position. Keeping the torso upright, wrists stacked above elbows, and driving the weight in a straight vertical path avoids all three.
