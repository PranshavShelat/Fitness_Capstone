Exercise: Hip Thrust
Category: Legs (glutes, hamstrings)
Type: Compound, barbell

The hip thrust is the most direct glute-building movement available, loading the glutes through their full range at the top of the hip-extension pattern in a way squats and deadlifts only partially do. It fits well on leg day alongside a quad-dominant lift (squats) and a hamstring-dominant lift (Romanian deadlift).

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 15-20 reps per set, 3 sets, moderate rest (60s).
- Maintaining: 10-15 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 6-10 reps per set with heavier loads, 4 sets, longer rest (90s).

Common form cue: drive through the heels and squeeze the glutes hard at the top rather than hyperextending the lower back to reach lockout - the movement should end with the hips fully extended and ribs down, not an arched back.
