Exercise: Pushups
Category: Push (chest, front shoulders, triceps)
Type: Compound, bodyweight

Pushups are the core bodyweight chest-pressing movement and belong on every push day, scaling from knee pushups for beginners to elevated-feet or weighted variations for advanced lifters.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 15-20 reps per set, 3-4 sets, short rest (45-60s).
- Maintaining: 10-15 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 8-12 reps per set (slow the tempo or elevate feet if bodyweight allows more than 15-20 reps), 4 sets, longer rest (90s).

Form risk this app watches for: the hips sagging below a straight line (loading the lower back instead of the core/shoulders) and the elbows flaring out wide from the torso, which rotates the shoulder into an impingement-prone position. Keeping a straight body line and elbows at roughly a 45-degree angle from the torso avoids both.
