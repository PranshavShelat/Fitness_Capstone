Exercise: Tricep Dips
Category: Push (triceps, chest, front shoulders)
Type: Compound, bodyweight

Dips are a compound pushing movement that loads the triceps hard while also involving the chest and front shoulders, making them a strong accessory or even primary movement on push day.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 15-20 reps per set, 3 sets, short rest (45-60s).
- Maintaining: 10-15 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 8-12 reps per set, 4 sets, adding a weighted vest or pause reps if bodyweight becomes too easy, longer rest (90s).

Form risk this app watches for: descending so deep the shoulder joint passes a well-supported range, which is linked to anterior shoulder instability and impingement. Stopping the descent once the upper arm is roughly parallel to the floor avoids this.
