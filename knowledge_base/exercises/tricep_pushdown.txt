Exercise: Tricep Pushdown
Category: Push accessory (triceps)
Type: Isolation, cable

The pushdown isolates the triceps directly and is a standard finisher on push day after the main pressing compounds, using a cable machine with a bar or rope attachment.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 15-20 reps per set, 3 sets, short rest (30-45s).
- Maintaining: 10-15 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 8-12 reps per set, 3-4 sets.

Common form cue: keep the elbows pinned at the sides throughout - letting them drift forward turns the movement into more of a chest press and takes tension off the triceps.
