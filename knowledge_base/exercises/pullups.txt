Exercise: Pullups
Category: Pull (lats, upper back, biceps)
Type: Compound, bodyweight

Pullups are the primary bodyweight back-pulling movement, building the lats and upper back while also loading the biceps as a secondary mover. They anchor any pull day.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: as many reps as possible per set (bodyweight pullups are already demanding), 3-4 sets, short rest (60s), assisted-band variation if full reps aren't yet possible.
- Maintaining: 6-10 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 5-8 reps per set with added weight if possible, 4 sets, longer rest (90-120s) since this is a demanding compound lift.

Pullups are generally lower injury-risk than the pressing movements when full range of motion is used and momentum/kipping is avoided; controlled reps with a full arm extension at the bottom keep the shoulder in a stable position.
