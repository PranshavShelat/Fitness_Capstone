Exercise: Lateral Raises
Category: Push accessory (side/medial shoulder)
Type: Isolation, dumbbell

Lateral raises isolate the medial deltoid, the muscle responsible for shoulder width, which barely gets worked by pressing movements. They're best placed after the main pressing compound lift on push/shoulder day.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 15-20 reps per set, 3 sets, light weight, short rest.
- Maintaining: 12-15 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 10-12 reps per set with moderate weight, 3-4 sets - kept light-to-moderate relative to compound lifts since this is a small, easily-irritated joint.

Form risk this app watches for: raising the arms above shoulder height, which narrows the space under the acromion and is a well-known impingement mechanism. Stopping the raise at shoulder height, not higher, protects the joint.
