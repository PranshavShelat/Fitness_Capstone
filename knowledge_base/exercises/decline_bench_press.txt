Exercise: Decline Bench Press
Category: Push (lower chest, triceps)
Type: Compound, barbell or dumbbell

The decline press shifts emphasis onto the lower chest, rounding out chest development alongside flat and incline pressing on the same push day.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 12-15 reps per set, 2-3 sets, moderate rest (60s).
- Maintaining: 8-12 reps per set, 2-3 sets.
- Bulking: 6-10 reps per set, 3 sets, longer rest (90s).

Common form cue: keep the movement controlled on the way down - the shorter range of motion at a decline angle makes it easy to turn the bottom of the rep into an uncontrolled bounce off the chest rather than a controlled stretch.
