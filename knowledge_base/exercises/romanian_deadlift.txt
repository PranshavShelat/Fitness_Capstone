Exercise: Romanian Deadlift
Category: Legs (hamstrings, glutes)
Type: Compound, barbell or dumbbell

The Romanian deadlift (RDL) isolates the hamstrings and glutes through a hip-hinge with minimal knee bend, complementing quad-dominant movements like squats on the same leg day - together they cover both major lower-body movement patterns.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 12-15 reps per set, 3 sets, moderate rest (60-90s).
- Maintaining: 8-12 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 6-10 reps per set with heavier loads, 3-4 sets, longer rest (90-120s).

Common form cue: hinge at the hips with a soft knee bend and a flat back, lowering the weight only as far as flexibility allows while keeping the bar close to the legs - rounding the back to chase extra range of motion is the main risk here.
