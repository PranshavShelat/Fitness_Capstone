Exercise: Leg Extension
Category: Legs accessory (quads)
Type: Isolation, machine

The leg extension isolates the quads directly, giving them focused volume that compound lifts (squats, deadlifts) share with other muscle groups. It's best placed after the main compound leg lifts as an accessory/finisher.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 15-20 reps per set, 3 sets, short rest (45s).
- Maintaining: 12-15 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 10-12 reps per set with moderate weight, 3 sets.

Common form cue: control the weight on the way down instead of letting it drop - the quads do most of their work eccentrically (lengthening under load) on this exercise, so a fast, uncontrolled negative wastes most of the benefit.
