Exercise: Chest Fly Machine
Category: Push accessory (chest)
Type: Isolation, machine

The chest fly isolates the chest through a stretch-and-squeeze motion that pressing movements don't emphasize as directly, making it a strong finisher on push/chest day after the main pressing lifts.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 15-20 reps per set, 3 sets, short rest (45s).
- Maintaining: 12-15 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 10-12 reps per set with moderate weight, 3 sets.

Common form cue: keep a slight bend in the elbows throughout and focus on squeezing the chest at the peak of the movement, rather than turning it into a second pressing exercise by bending the elbows more as the weight gets heavier.
