Exercise: Bench Press
Category: Push (chest, front shoulders, triceps)
Type: Compound, barbell or dumbbell

The flat bench press is the primary horizontal pressing movement for chest development and belongs on any push/chest day as a main lift, done before isolation accessories like chest fly or tricep pushdown.

Rep guidance by goal:
- Cutting: 12-15 reps per set, 3-4 sets, moderate rest (60s).
- Maintaining: 8-12 reps per set, 3 sets.
- Bulking: 6-10 reps per set with progressively heavier loads, 4 sets, longer rest (90-120s).

Common form cue: keep the shoulder blades pulled back and down against the bench throughout the lift, rather than letting the shoulders roll forward - this keeps the load on the chest and protects the shoulder joint from impingement.
