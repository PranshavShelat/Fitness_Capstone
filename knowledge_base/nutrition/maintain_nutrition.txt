Nutrition strategy: Maintaining (stable weight)

Eat at roughly estimated maintenance calories, with protein around 1.6-1.8g per kg of bodyweight to support recovery and preserve muscle without needing a surplus. Carbohydrates and fats can be balanced more flexibly than in a cut or bulk since the priority is consistency, not pushing in either direction.

Meal pattern: 3 balanced meals plus 1 snack, each meal built around a palm-sized protein source, a fist-sized carb source, and a serving of vegetables or fruit, with a thumb-sized portion of fats (nuts, oil, avocado). This "balanced plate" approach is easy to sustain day to day without needing to track every gram, which suits a maintenance goal.
