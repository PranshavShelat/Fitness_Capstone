Nutrition strategy: Cutting (fat loss)

Run a moderate calorie deficit (roughly 15-20% below maintenance) rather than an aggressive one, since a deficit that's too steep risks losing muscle along with fat and is harder to sustain for a full week. Protein should be kept high, around 1.8-2.2g per kg of bodyweight, to protect lean mass while in a deficit - this is the single most important macro to prioritize while cutting. Carbohydrates and fats fill the remaining calories, with carbs typically favored around training sessions for performance and fats kept moderate.

Meal pattern: 3 main meals plus 1-2 protein-forward snacks (Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, a protein shake) work well to hit the higher protein target without excess calories. Favor high-volume, lower-calorie-density foods (vegetables, lean proteins, whole grains) to stay full on fewer calories. Lean protein sources: chicken breast, white fish, egg whites, tofu, low-fat Greek yogurt. Lower-calorie carb sources: rice, oats, potatoes, fruit, vegetables.
