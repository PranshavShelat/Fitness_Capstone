Nutrition strategy: Bulking (muscle gain)

Run a moderate calorie surplus (roughly 10-15% above maintenance) rather than a large one, since an excessive surplus mostly adds fat rather than extra muscle. Protein should stay around 1.6-2.0g per kg of bodyweight - more isn't needed once a surplus is already supporting growth. The extra calories should come primarily from carbohydrates, which fuel harder training sessions and support recovery, with fats kept at a moderate, sufficient level.

Meal pattern: 4-5 meals across the day (3 main meals plus 2 snacks) makes it easier to hit a higher calorie target without large, uncomfortable meals. Calorie-dense but still nutritious foods work well: rice, oats, potatoes, whole milk, nut butters, and olive oil alongside the usual lean proteins, plus a post-workout meal or shake with both protein and carbs to support recovery.
