BMI context: Overweight or Obese (BMI 25 and above)

Someone in this BMI category who wants to bulk further has little room to do so safely - even if they've selected a "bulk" goal, the plan should lean toward at most a maintenance-level intake rather than an additional surplus, since adding more weight from an already-elevated BMI carries more risk than benefit; a slight deficit or recomposition-style approach (moderate protein, resistance training, small deficit) is generally more appropriate. If the goal is cut or maintain, standard guidance applies without adjustment, and starting with a moderate rather than aggressive deficit is still preferred for sustainability.
