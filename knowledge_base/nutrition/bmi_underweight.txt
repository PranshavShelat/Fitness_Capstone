BMI context: Underweight (BMI under 18.5)

Someone in this BMI category who wants to cut further has little room to do so safely - even if they've selected a "cut" goal, the plan should lean toward at most a maintenance-level intake rather than an additional deficit, since further weight loss from an already-low BMI carries more risk than benefit. If the goal is bulk or maintain, standard guidance applies without adjustment. Emphasizing nutrient-dense, calorie-sufficient meals matters more than any specific macro split at this BMI.
