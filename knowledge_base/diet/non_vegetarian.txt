Dietary preference: Non-Vegetarian

Meat, poultry, fish, eggs, and dairy are all available, giving the widest and most calorie-efficient set of complete protein sources - each provides all essential amino acids in one food, so hitting a high daily protein target (important for both cutting, where protein protects muscle in a deficit, and bulking, where it supports growth) takes fewer, simpler meals than a restricted diet needs.

Go-to protein sources: chicken breast, turkey, lean beef, white fish, salmon, shrimp, eggs and egg whites, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, whey protein. For a cut, favor the leaner end of this list (chicken breast, white fish, egg whites, low-fat dairy) to keep calories down while protein stays high. For a bulk, fattier cuts (salmon, whole eggs, higher-fat dairy) can help hit the higher calorie target without needing huge food volume. Meals may freely include any of the above - there is no restriction to work around here.
