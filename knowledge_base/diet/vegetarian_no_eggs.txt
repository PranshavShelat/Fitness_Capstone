Dietary preference: Vegetarian (no eggs, no meat/poultry/fish)

With both meat and eggs excluded, every meal has to build its protein from dairy and plant sources, which are individually less protein-dense and often missing one or more essential amino acids - so hitting a high daily protein target (still important for both cutting and bulking) takes more deliberate planning: more frequent protein-forward meals/snacks, and combining sources within the same meal rather than relying on one dominant food.

Go-to protein sources: Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, paneer, milk, tofu, tempeh, seitan, lentils, chickpeas, black beans, edamame, quinoa, nuts and nut butters, chia and hemp seeds, whey (if dairy is acceptable) or pea/soy protein powder. No meat, poultry, fish, or eggs should ever appear in a meal for this diet, including as a hidden ingredient. Pairing a grain with a legume (e.g. rice and lentils, or whole-wheat bread and chickpeas) or dairy with a legume (e.g. yogurt and beans) gives a more complete amino acid profile than either alone. Protein powder is a practical way to close the gap on high-protein days (cutting or bulking) without needing unrealistically large meals.
