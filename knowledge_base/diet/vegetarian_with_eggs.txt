Dietary preference: Vegetarian (eats eggs, no meat/poultry/fish)

Eggs are a complete protein and, along with dairy, make hitting a high daily protein target considerably easier than for a vegetarian who excludes them - a couple of eggs plus a dairy or legume source per meal gets close to what a non-vegetarian gets from a single serving of meat, without needing much more food volume.

Go-to protein sources: eggs and egg whites, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, paneer, milk, tofu, tempeh, lentils, chickpeas, black beans, edamame, quinoa, whey or pea protein powder. No meat, poultry, or fish should ever appear in a meal for this diet - not even "just a little" for flavor or convenience. For a cut, egg whites and low-fat dairy keep protein high without excess calories; whole eggs and full-fat dairy/paneer help hit calorie targets more comfortably during a bulk. Combining a grain or legume with dairy or eggs in the same meal (e.g. rice and paneer, or lentils and yogurt) rounds out the amino acid profile even further.
